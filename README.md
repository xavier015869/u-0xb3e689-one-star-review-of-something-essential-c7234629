## One-Star Review of Something Essential

A single deadpan one-star review of sleep, written for make.fun's
**One-Star Review of Something Essential** bounty. The entrypoint
scores six candidate reviews against the judge's five criteria, picks
the winner, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, 93
characters, inside the 160-character limit).

> Sleep: I pay eight hours a night and wake up tired. No refund, no customer service, one star.

Why this line: sleep is the essential everyone relies on, and the
sharpest true grievance against it is that you pay it a third of your
life and still wake up tired. The complaint is filed in earnest, flat
throughout, and the sting lands in the last few words — a customer
service complaint capped by the verdict itself.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts   # writes the review to /output/entry.txt
```

No dependencies, no network — just Node and the standard library.
